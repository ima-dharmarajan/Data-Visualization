# Social Media & Mental Health Analysis

# Project Overview
This project explores the relationship between social media usage and mental well-being through an interactive Power BI dashboard. The dashboard analyzes screen time, sleep patterns, mood, stress, anxiety, and social media engagement to identify patterns and provide meaningful insights.

# Objectives
- Analyze social media usage and screen time patterns.
- Explore the relationship between screen time and sleep quality.
- Understand mood distribution across different genders.
- Analyze stress and anxiety in relation to social media usage.
- Identify patterns across age groups and social media platforms.
  
# Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization

# Dashboard Pages

1. Home :
    The landing page introducing the Social Media & Mental Health Analysis dashboard and providing navigation to the different analytical sections.

2. Overview :
    Presents an overall summary of social media usage and mental health indicators, offering a quick understanding of the dataset and its key metrics.

3. Mental Distribution & Time :
    Explores mental health patterns through mood distribution across genders, mental state categories, and activity levels across different age groups. It helps      understand how mental well-being indicators vary among different user segments.

4. Social Media Impact :
    Analyzes social media usage across different platforms, including screen time and interaction patterns. It also explores the relationship between screen time,   stress, and anxiety levels.

5. Wellness & Risk :
   Focuses on wellness indicators, including sleep quality and mental health risk patterns. It examines the relationship between screen time and sleep duration to understand potential wellness concerns.
